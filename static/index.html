What goes with a higher $ psf?

Spearman ρ with each block's median $ psf

Across Singapore compares every block, so it mixes in the big premium for central towns. Within the same town compares blocks only against their own town, then averages across towns (weighted by block count), which shows what matters once location is held roughly constant. For distances and minutes, a negative ρ means closer or quicker goes with pricier. The Value score is left out because it's computed from $ psf, and the CBD and Lease scores are shown via their raw measures (commute minutes, lease years), which they mirror exactly.
